    THE ARCHIVE

    Football has a geography.

    Ajax Atlas connects the places players were born with their senior club careers. Inspired by the linked map and timeline of Paris, building by building.

    Coverage, not a claim of completeness

    The roster is Wikipedia’s List of AFC Ajax players, restricted to careers overlapping 1950–2025. It may include contracted players without senior appearances and omit others. This is not an independently audited first-team appearance register. Dates, birthplaces and club spells come from linked player articles. Missing records remain searchable.

    Reading the map

    Birthplace dots are settlement centres, not exact birth addresses. Shared locations are stacked; clicking a cluster opens its player list. Club dots use coordinates on club articles or explicitly linked Wikidata stadiums, usually stadium locations, which may reflect a current rather than historical ground. Missing club coordinates do not remove the club from the career list. Lines are schematic connections, not travelled routes.

    Reading time

    The year filter selects players with an Ajax spell overlapping that calendar year, not a season squad. All years shows 1950–2025. In 3D timeline view, each red dot is a player’s first recorded Ajax year (pre-1950 arrivals are placed at 1950), its height is birthplace latitude and its depth is longitude. Unknown locations are omitted from 3D views. Club histories are clipped at 2025. National teams, youth clubs and coaching roles are excluded.
